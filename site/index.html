From list to route

  1. Add your list and starting address.

    Confirm the address match and review any unfamiliar item categories.

  2. Find your store order.

    The planner compares road travel times across up to three nearby stores per category.

  3. Open Google Maps.

    One button sends the ordered stops, including a return to your start if selected. Google Maps may show a route preview before navigation.

Public mapping services can be temporarily unavailable. Your address and coordinates go to Photon. Coordinates also go to public Overpass backup services (VK Maps, Private.coffee or overpass-api.de) and OSRM. The Google Maps link includes your starting point and stops.
